About us

About Us : CMJ REFRACTORIES & MINERALS PVT. LTD. had been in operation for more than a decade as a part of CMJ Group, under the able leadership of Mr. Anup Kumar Jain, & Mr. Rahul Jain. We are catering to the requirement of basic & service industries with REFRACTORY / INSULATION Service towards Steel Plant, Cement Plant etc. along with supplies of raw materials, semi-finished and finished goods. We also undertake Turnkey projects on establishing plants & equipments in various countries. Similar Job was done in GCC and African Countries. Innovation, Research and Development, commercialization of in-house developed technology are the motto of CMJ Group

Mission : As a leading Manufacturers & Exporters, we provide wide range of refractory products and solutions that demonstrate superior value to our clients in the Steel, Aluminum, Cement, Glass and Petrochemicals Industries. We also developed new refractory products to meet individual customers needs. Partner with our customers to improve their operations and profitability. Our success is based on providing solutions for our Customers process.

Quality Control : The production process is closely monitored under stringent quality control by our laboratory. Intensive in house R&D keeps and company committed to continuously upgrade product quality and also steer ahead in refractories provide the customers the latest.

Services : A team of highly trained field sales and service force to assist with product selection, trials, installation and after sales support. Development facilities focused on developing new products to meet individual customers refractory requirements.
