About Us

CMJ SERVICES Pvt. Ltd. had been in operation for more than a decade as a part of CMJ Group, under the able leadership of Mr. Rahul Jain, Mrs. Namrata Jain. We are catering to the requirement of basic & service industries with REFRACTORY / INSULATION Service towards Steel Plant, Cement Plant etc. along with supplies of raw materials, semi-finished and finished goods. We also undertake Turnkey projects on establishing plants & equipments in various countries. Similar Job was done in GCC and African Countries. Innovation, Research and Development, commercialization of in-house developed technology are the motto of CMJ Group

Our Mission

Our Company carries out Refractory Consultancy, Refractory Engineering and Refractory Management activities within the scope of project execution and also offers these as separate services including Refractory Installation & Commissioning. The result Our professional Technical Team is catering to the needs of tomorrows projects in the light of emerging technologies.

Our Services

A team of highly trained field sales and service force to assist with product selection, trials, installation and after sales support. Development facilities focused on developing new products to meet individual customers refractory requirements.
